A short apprenticeship

The Strokes

I.

The Swing

Your racket shadows the mouse. Sweep through the ball — the faster the swing, the heavier the pace. Sweep left or right to place it into the corners.

II.

Topspin

Brush upward through contact. The ball arcs generously over the net, dips sharply, and kicks off the turf. Hit it as hard as you dare.

III.

The Slice

Carve downward through the ball. It floats, holds its line, and skids through low off the grass — maddening for anyone standing on the other side.

IV.

The Drop Shot

The gentlest touch — barely move at all as you meet the ball — and it dies just beyond the net while the professional is still ambling forward.

V.

The Serve

Click to toss, then sweep up through the ball — it always finds the box. Swing speed is pace; strike at the top of the toss for a flat thunderbolt, or meet it softer and lower for a kicking spin serve.

VI.

Etiquette

The crowd hushes for your serve and applauds a rally well fought. The birds, it must be said, carry on regardless. Sound may be silenced from the club nav.
